Forgotten Island
Forgotten Island follows two inseparable Filipino teens whose bond is tested when they stumble into Nakali, a mythical realm from local folklore, and must navigate its wild inhabitants to find a way home before parting for good. With dazzling animation blending anime flourishes, 1990s nostalgia, and rich cultural detail, the film offers a heartfelt portrait of female friendship. Critics praised its inventive worldbuilding, humor, and emotional depth, calling it one of DreamWorks’ most delightful recent works.
